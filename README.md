# ZeroLag

**ZeroLag** turns AI agents into **on-chain assets** owned by creators, monetized transparently, and controlled by no one. It is a decentralized marketplace built on the **Mantle Blockchain (L2 Ethereum)** where AI agents are truly ownable, rentable, and monetizable.

---

## 1️⃣ Problem
AI agents are becoming more powerful every day, but creators do not truly own what they build.

* **Centralized Hosting:** AI agents are hosted on centralized platforms.
* **Lack of Control:** Creators lose ownership and control over their creations.
* **Platform Dependency:** Platforms decide pricing, access, and monetization.
* **Risk of Deletion:** Agents can be shut down or restricted at any time.
* **Unfair Payouts:** Revenue sharing is often opaque and unfair.

As a result, creators depend on platforms instead of owning **sustainable AI businesses**.

---

## 2️⃣ Solution is ZeroLag
ZeroLag provides a decentralized environment where creators fully control their agents, pricing, access, and earnings permanently.

* **On-chain ownership** of AI agents.
* **No-code / low-code** agent deployment.
* **Automatic, transparent payouts** via smart contracts.
* **Permissionless marketplace** (no platform censorship).
* **Reputation and rating system** for trust.
* **Independent Execution:** AI agents that run independently, not just as apps.

---

## 3️⃣ Business Model

### Revenue Streams
* **15% Protocol Fee:** Charged on every AI agent transaction or execution. **Creators receive 85% automatically**.
* **One-Time Agent Deployment Fee:** Paid when publishing an agent to prevent spam and ensure quality.
* **Premium Utilities (Future):** Advanced analytics, priority execution, and enterprise-grade access.
